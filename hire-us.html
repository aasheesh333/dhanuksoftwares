Good to know

Questions people ask us

How much does a website cost?

Depends on what you need — a simple business site and a big custom one are very different. Message us what you're thinking and we'll give you a fixed quote within 24 hours. No charge for the quote, obviously.

How fast can you deliver?

Landing pages usually go live in 2–3 days, business websites in about a week. Apps take longer — typically 3–6 weeks depending on features. We'll give you an exact date in the quote and we stick to it.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Everything happens over WhatsApp/email/video call, and we've set up our workflow for remote clients. Timelines and quotes work the same.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small fixes right after launch are on us. For ongoing updates we offer simple monthly care plans — or you can just ping us when you need something and we'll quote it.

Who owns the website/app once it's done?

You do. Full ownership — domain, code, Play Store listing, everything transferred to you. We're not into lock-in.
